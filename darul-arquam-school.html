Our Story

About Darul Arquam

Founded with a vision to provide quality English-medium education enriched with Islamic values, Darul Arquam Public School has grown to become one of Shajapur's most trusted educational institutions. Our school takes its name from the blessed house of Al-Arquam ibn Abi Al-Arquam — the first school of Islam — embodying our commitment to learning as an act of worship.

Over 19 years, we have nurtured thousands of students who have gone on to excel in medicine, engineering, commerce, Islamic scholarship, and public service — carrying with them both the knowledge and the values we instilled.

🌟 Our Vision

To be a model institution that produces academically excellent, morally upright, and spiritually grounded citizens who contribute to society with knowledge and integrity.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide holistic education that integrates modern knowledge with Islamic wisdom, developing every student's intellectual, physical, and spiritual potential.

Bismillah. It is my great honour to welcome you to Darul Arquam Public School. Education is not merely the transfer of information — it is the illumination of the soul and the cultivation of character. At Darul Arquam, we strive every day to be a place where knowledge flourishes alongside faith.

Our dedicated team of educators works with passion to ensure that every child in our care reaches their fullest potential — academically, spiritually, and as a human being. We believe that an educated Muslim is the greatest asset to any community.

I invite every family to join our Darul Arquam family — where your child's future begins with بسم الله.

Maulana Abdul Hamid Saheb Principal, Darul Arquam Public School

🤝 Islamic Values & Educational Philosophy

We follow the Prophetic model — "Seek knowledge from the cradle to the grave." Every subject is taught with an awareness of its place in Allah's creation. We produce students who are not only smart, but wise; not only knowledgeable, but righteous.

Admissions 2024–2025

Simple, transparent, and welcoming — we make the admission process easy for every family.

1

Enquire Online

Fill our online form or visit the school office to express your interest.

2

Entrance Test

Students above Class 1 appear for a simple assessment test.

3

Document Verification

Submit required documents for verification at the school office.

4

Fee Payment & Enroll

Pay the admission fee and receive your ward's class schedule.

📋 Required Documents

  • Birth Certificate (original + photocopy)
  • Previous school's Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Last year's Report Card / Mark Sheet
  • Aadhar Card of Student and Parents
  • 4 Passport Size Photographs (recent)
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Address Proof (Electricity Bill / Voter ID)
  • Income Certificate (for scholarship eligibility)

📞 Eligibility Criteria

Admissions open for Nursery (age 3+) through Class 10. Preference is given to applicants from Shajapur and surrounding areas. All religious communities are welcome — we nurture character and knowledge in every child.
